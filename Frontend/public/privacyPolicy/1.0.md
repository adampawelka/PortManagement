## 4. How We Collect Personal Data

Personal data is collected through the following methods:

- **Direct Input:** Port Authority Officers register shipping agents, representatives, and staff members through system forms
- **User Submission:** Shipping Agent Representatives submit vessel visit notifications containing crew information
- **Authentication Provider:** Auth0 provides user identity information upon login
- **Automatic Collection:** System generates audit logs automatically during user interactions
- **Activation Process:** Users provide confirmation through activation links sent to their email

| Data Type                       | Legal Basis                                     | Description                                                |

## 7. Data Retention

| Data Category            | Retention Period                      | Justification                             |
|--------------------------|---------------------------------------|-------------------------------------------|
| Shipping Agent Records   | 7 years after last activity           | Maritime compliance requirements          |
| Staff Records            | 5 years after employment/contract end | Labor law and audit requirements          |
| Vessel Visit Data        | 7 years                               | Maritime and customs regulations          |

After retention periods expire, personal data is either securely deleted or anonymized.

## 9. Data Subject Rights

Under GDPR, you have the following rights:

### 9.1 Right to be Informed (Article 13 and 14)
Request information when your personal data is obtained or not.

### 9.2 Right to Access (Article 15)
Request a copy of your personal data we hold.

### 9.3 Right to Rectification (Article 16)
Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete personal data.

### 9.4 Right to Erasure / "Right to be Forgotten" (Article 17)
Request deletion of your personal data (subject to legal retention obligations).

### 9.5 Right to Restrict Processing (Article 18)
Request limitation of how we process your data.

### 9.6 Right to Data Portability (Article 20)
Receive your personal data in a structured, machine-readable format.

### 9.7 Right to Object (Article 21)
Object to processing based on legitimate interests.

### 9.8 Rights Related to Automated Decision-Making (Article 22)
Our system uses algorithms for scheduling and dock assignment. You have the right to request human review of automated decisions.

**To exercise your rights, contact us.**

We will respond to requests within 30 days.

## 10. Security Measures

We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect personal data:

- **Encryption:** Data encrypted in transit (TLS/SSL) and at rest
- **Access Controls:** Role-based access control (RBAC) and attribute-based access control (ABAC)
- **Authentication:** Secure authentication via Auth0 with multi-factor authentication support
- **Audit Logging:** Comprehensive logging of all data access and modifications
- **Network Security:** Access restricted to DEI internal network or VPN (development/staging environments)
- **Regular Security Assessments:** Periodic security audits and vulnerability testing
- **Data Backup:** Regular encrypted backups with defined Recovery Point Objectives (RPO)
- **Incident Response:** Documented procedures for data breach notification
