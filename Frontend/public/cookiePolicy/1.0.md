# Cookie Policy

**Effective Date:** December 23, 2025  
**Version:** 1.0

## 1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files placed on your device (computer, smartphone, or tablet) when you visit a website. They allow the website to recognize your device and remember information about your visit, such as your preferences and login status.

## 2. How We Use Cookies

The Port Management System uses cookies and similar technologies to:
- Maintain your authenticated session
- Remember your language preference
- Protect against security threats
- Improve system performance
- Analyze how users interact with the System

## 3. Types of Cookies We Use

### 3.1 Essential Cookies (Always Active)

These cookies are necessary for the System to function and cannot be disabled.

| Cookie Name | Purpose | Duration | Set By |
|-------------|---------|----------|--------|
| `auth0.session` | Maintains your authenticated session after login via Auth0 | Session | Auth0 |
| `auth0.is.authenticated` | Indicates authentication status | Session | Auth0 |
| `auth_token` | Secure authentication token | Session | Our System |
| `csrf_token` | Protection against Cross-Site Request Forgery attacks | Session | Our System |
| `session_id` | Unique identifier for your current session | Session | Our System |

**Why These Are Essential:**
- Without these cookies, you cannot log in or use the System
- They ensure secure communication between your browser and our servers
- They protect against unauthorized access and security attacks

### 3.2 Functional Cookies (Can Be Disabled)

These cookies enhance your experience but are not strictly necessary.

| Cookie Name | Purpose | Duration | Set By |
|-------------|---------|----------|--------|
| `user_language` | Remembers your language preference (EN/PT) | 1 year | Our System |
| `ui_preferences` | Stores your interface customization settings | 1 year | Our System |
| `sidebar_collapsed` | Remembers sidebar state (expanded/collapsed) | 30 days | Our System |
| `theme_preference` | Stores your theme preference (if available) | 1 year | Our System |

**Impact of Disabling:**
- You will need to re-select your language on each visit
- Your UI preferences will not be saved between sessions
- The System will work, but with a less personalized experience

### 3.3 Analytics Cookies (Optional)

These cookies help us understand how users interact with the System.

| Cookie Name | Purpose | Duration | Set By |
|-------------|---------|----------|--------|
| `_ga` | Google Analytics - distinguishes users | 2 years | Google Analytics |
| `_gid` | Google Analytics - distinguishes users | 24 hours | Google Analytics |
| `_gat` | Google Analytics - throttles request rate | 1 minute | Google Analytics |

**What We Track:**
- Pages visited and features used
- Time spent on different sections
- Navigation patterns and user flows
- Error rates and system performance
- Anonymized demographic information

**Privacy Note:**
- Analytics data is anonymized and aggregated
- We cannot identify individual users from this data
- IP addresses are anonymized before storage

## 5. Cookie Lifespan

### 5.1 Session Cookies
- **Duration:** Deleted when you close your browser
- **Purpose:** Maintain your active session while using the System
- **Examples:** Authentication tokens, CSRF protection

### 5.2 Persistent Cookies
- **Duration:** Remain on your device for a set period
- **Purpose:** Remember preferences between sessions
- **Examples:** Language preference (1 year), UI settings (1 year)

### 5.3 Expiration Policy
All cookies have defined expiration periods. Once expired, they are automatically deleted by your browser.

## 6. Managing Your Cookie Preferences

### 6.1 Cookie Consent Banner

When you first visit the System, you will see a cookie consent banner with the following options:

- **Accept All:** Enables all cookies including analytics
- **Reject Non-Essential:** Only essential cookies are enabled
- **Customize:** Choose which cookie categories to enable

Your preference is stored and respected on future visits.

### 6.2 Changing Your Preferences

You can modify your cookie preferences at any time:

1. **Via System Settings:**
   - Log in to the System
   - Navigate to "Account Settings" → "Privacy & Cookies"
   - Update your cookie preferences

2. **Via Footer Link:**
   - Click "Cookie Settings" in the page footer
   - Update your preferences in the pop-up dialog

### 6.3 Browser Settings

You can also control cookies through your browser settings:

**Google Chrome:**
1. Click the three dots (⋮) → Settings
2. Privacy and security → Cookies and other site data
3. Configure your cookie preferences

**Mozilla Firefox:**
1. Click the menu (≡) → Settings
2. Privacy & Security
3. Configure cookie settings under "Cookies and Site Data"

**Microsoft Edge:**
1. Click the three dots (…) → Settings
2. Cookies and site permissions → Cookies and site data
3. Configure your preferences

**Safari:**
1. Safari menu → Preferences
2. Privacy tab
3. Configure cookie settings

**Note:** Blocking all cookies may prevent you from using the System entirely.

## 7. Do Not Track (DNT)

Some browsers offer a "Do Not Track" (DNT) signal. Currently, there is no industry standard for how websites should respond to DNT signals. We do not alter our data collection practices based on DNT signals, but you can control cookies through the methods described above.

## 8. Mobile Devices

## 9. Consequences of Disabling Cookies

### 9.1 Disabling Essential Cookies
**Impact:**
- You cannot log in or authenticate
- The System will not function
- Security protections will be compromised

**Recommendation:** Essential cookies must remain enabled.

### 9.2 Disabling Functional Cookies
**Impact:**
- Language preference not saved (defaults to English)
- UI customizations reset on each visit
- Less personalized user experience

**Recommendation:** Enable for better user experience.

### 9.3 Disabling Analytics Cookies
**Impact:**
- No impact on System functionality
- We cannot improve the System based on usage patterns
- Your usage data will not contribute to analytics

**Recommendation:** Your choice - no functional impact.
